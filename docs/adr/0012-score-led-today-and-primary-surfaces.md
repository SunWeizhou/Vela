# Make Today score-led and organize the product around Today, Trends, Plan, and Coach

Vela will use four primary surfaces: Today, Trends, Plan, and Coach. Today gives the five independent scored results the primary visual role through a stable 3+2 hierarchy: Recovery, Sleep, and Strain use circular gauges; Physiological Stress uses a current value with a compact time-series trace; Energy uses a reserve/battery gauge. It will not show a total score or a large Rhythm Horizon hero. Trends owns time-series, Personal Baseline, and deviation exploration; Plan owns the editable Daily Operating Plan; Coach owns explanation, follow-up, artifacts, and memory. Training remains a downstream capability inside Plan and Coach, with in-session execution left to Apple Watch and system workout apps. This trades a feature-category navigation model for a stable daily decision model, reduces explanatory clutter, and preserves direct access to evidence and AI reasoning one level deeper.
