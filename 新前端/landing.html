Readiness that means something

Most apps give you a single score. Vela breaks down why: sleep quality, HRV trend, resting heart rate, and recent strain all feed into a readiness ring you can actually act on.

⚡

Training that adapts

Your calendar isn't static. When your recovery dips, Vela adjusts tomorrow's volume automatically. Original plan crossed out, adapted plan below — always transparent, never opaque.

💡

Memory that learns you

Vela notices patterns you'd miss: late-night eating tanks your deep sleep, Zone 2 runs boost next-day HRV. Each discovery lands in your Memory Inbox — confirm, modify, or ignore.

Privacy by architecture

All health data stays on-device. Coach recommendations are computed locally. Your data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose to share anonymized model metrics.
